# pepe.coffee Tip Button

Browser extension for Chrome: tip creators in Pepecoin (PEP) right where you see them, on Reddit, X, Twitch, Kick, Lichess, GitHub, Bitcointalk, Medium, YouTube, Rumble, AniList, MyAnimeList and Smogon.

**Get it:** [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/oebkggcoblkkkijfoejifakdonhohbje) · [pepe.coffee](https://pepe.coffee)

## Why the code is public

So you can check what the extension does with your coins and your login.

- Your password never leaves your device. The wallet key is derived in your browser and never sent anywhere.
- Payments are signed in your browser. The server only receives the finished, signed transaction.
- The extension talks to `pepe.coffee` only when you click a Tip or Airdrop button. It does not track what you read or watch.
- Permissions: `storage` (keeps you logged in) and the websites listed in `manifest.json`.

pepe.coffee is free and non-custodial. It never holds your coins.
